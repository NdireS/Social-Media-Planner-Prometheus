# Platform playbook (baseline: late 2026)

Platforms change features and limits all the time. At the start of every weekly run, search the web for
**"{platform} new features {month} {year}"** and **"{platform} algorithm update {year}"**, and add any
changes to the plan's *Platform notes* section. Treat the numbers below as defaults that you check, not as facts.

The rule: **never cross-post the same asset unchanged.** The idea can be shared across platforms, but its
expression must be native to each one.

---

## LinkedIn (Company Page)
- **Culture:** professional identity, career and business outcomes, credibility. People read on desktop at work and on mobile while commuting.
- **Formats that win:** document carousels (PDF, 1080×1350, 6 to 12 slides), text-led posts with a strong first 2 lines, native vertical or square video (under 90 s), polls (sparingly), newsletters (Pages can publish), employee reshares.
- **Specs:** text up to 3,000 characters, with the cut at about 210 characters ("…see more"), so the hook must fit before it. Images 1200×627 (landscape) or 1080×1350 (portrait). Video 9:16 or 1:1 or 16:9, with captions burned in.
- **Best practice:** 3 to 5 hashtags at most, placed at the end. Ask a specific question to earn comments. Reply within the first 60 minutes. Put outbound links in the first comment when you are testing reach. Tag people only when it's genuine.
- **Avoid:** engagement-bait phrasing, too many hashtags, consumer-style memes (unless the brand is playful).

## Facebook Page
- **Culture:** community, family, local life, groups, shares to friends. The audience skews older than Instagram's and TikTok's. In many markets, including Africa, South Asia and LatAm, Facebook is still the dominant platform.
- **Formats that win:** Reels (all new video is treated as a Reel), photo posts with a local, human feel, link posts with a strong preview, Lives, group posts, Stories.
- **Specs:** images 1080×1350 or 1080×1080. Reels 1080×1920, 9:16, with captions. Text: keep the hook under 125 characters (the visible area before truncation).
- **Best practice:** local references, shareable "send this to someone who…" posts, comment-reply conversations, 1 to 3 hashtags.
- **Avoid:** watermarked TikTok re-uploads, which get reduced distribution.

## Instagram
- **Culture:** aesthetic identity, aspiration, saves and sends (DM shares are a top signal). Reels drive discovery; carousels drive depth and saves.
- **Formats that win:** Reels (up to 3 min; the first 1.5 s decides retention), carousels (up to 20 slides, 1080×1350), Stories (polls, quizzes, links), Broadcast channels, Collabs (co-authored posts), Trial Reels (shown to non-followers first).
- **Specs:** feed 1080×1350 (4:5). The profile grid previews at 3:4, so keep key content in the centre. Reels and Stories 1080×1920 (keep the bottom 20 % and top 14 % free of text). Captions up to 2,200 characters, front-loaded with keywords (Instagram SEO).
- **Best practice:** a hook in on-screen text in the first frame, original audio or a trending sound chosen early, 3 to 5 targeted hashtags, a keyword-rich caption, alt text, and a "send this to…" CTA to drive shares.
- **Avoid:** TikTok watermarks, low-resolution uploads, a wall of hashtags.

## TikTok
- **Culture:** entertainment first, raw and authentic, creator-led, a search engine for younger audiences. The For You feed doesn't care about follower count.
- **Formats that win:** 15 to 60 s videos with a hook in the first second, longer 1 to 3 min explainers or storytimes for search, photo carousels (Photo Mode, swipe), Stitch and Duet, Lives, series and playlists, TikTok Notes.
- **Specs:** 1080×1920, 9:16. Keep text out of the right 15 % and the bottom 25 % (the UI covers it). Captions up to 4,000 characters, so use searchable keywords. On-screen text plus spoken keywords help search.
- **Best practice:** pattern interrupt in the first 0 to 1 s, fast cuts every 1 to 3 s, native captions, trending sounds at low volume under a voiceover, reply-to-comment videos, and 2 to 3 posts a day for growth.
- **Avoid:** polished ad aesthetics (they get skipped), horizontal video, slow intros.

## X
- **Culture:** real-time conversation, news, opinions, threads, communities. Replies and reposts are what matter most.
- **Formats that win:** short punchy posts, threads (5 to 12 posts), image and data posts, short native video, polls, quote-posts, Communities, Spaces.
- **Specs:** 280 characters for standard accounts (long posts for Premium). Images 1600×900 (16:9) or 1080×1350. Video up to 2:20 for standard accounts.
- **Best practice:** opinionated first lines, numbers, timely takes, replying to large accounts in the niche (that's the appearance quota), 0 to 2 hashtags, and posting several times a day.
- **Avoid:** hashtag stuffing, links in the main post when you are testing reach (put them in a reply).

## Bluesky
- **Culture:** a decentralised, conversation-first, anti-engagement-bait community. It values authenticity, alt text and expertise. Custom feeds and starter packs drive discovery.
- **Formats that win:** text posts of up to 300 characters, threads, up to 4 images, short video (up to 3 min), link cards (links are **not** penalised), quote-posts, and getting into topic custom feeds.
- **Specs:** 300 characters. Images 1080×1350 or 16:9. **Always** add alt text.
- **Best practice:** find and post into relevant custom feeds (often hashtag or keyword based), create a starter pack for the niche, reply generously, and keep a human, slightly witty tone.
- **Avoid:** copy-paste from X, corporate tone, growth-hack language. The community notices and dislikes it.

## YouTube
- **Culture:** search plus recommendations, long attention, deep trust. Shorts drive discovery; long-form builds authority and drives sales.
- **Formats that win:** Shorts (up to 3 min, 9:16), long-form (8 to 20 min) with chapters, Community posts (polls, images), Lives, Podcasts, Playlists, and "Related video" links from Shorts to long-form.
- **Specs:** long-form 1920×1080 (16:9), 4K if possible. Thumbnails 1280×720 (under 2 MB, readable at small size, 3 to 5 words at most). Shorts 1080×1920. Titles about 60 characters before truncation. The first 150 characters of the description matter.
- **Best practice:** a title that promises a payoff, a thumbnail that matches the title, a hook in the first 15 s (long-form) or 1 s (Shorts), chapters, pinned comment, end screens, and links from Shorts to long-form.
- **Avoid:** clickbait that the video doesn't deliver (it hurts retention), long intros.

## Telegram Channel
- **Culture:** direct, loyal, notification-driven. People open it because they chose to. It's popular in Africa, Eastern Europe, the Middle East and Asia, and for crypto, finance, education and news.
- **Formats that win:** text posts with formatting (bold, spoilers, quotes), image or video plus caption, polls and quizzes, voice notes and video messages (circles), files and PDFs, Channel Stories (with boosts), comments via a linked discussion group, and scheduled posts.
- **Specs:** text up to 4,096 characters. Media captions up to 1,024 characters (more for Premium). Images about 1280 px wide.
- **Best practice:** a consistent daily drop time, exclusive value (early access, a member-only tip), a summary-first structure, pinned "start here" post, 1 to 6 posts a day, emoji used as bullet structure, and silent notifications for minor posts.
- **Avoid:** forwarding the same post from other channels, unbroken walls of text.

## Native adaptation checklist (per post)
- [ ] Aspect ratio and safe zones are correct for the platform
- [ ] The hook is written for that platform's first-impression area
- [ ] Hashtag count and style match the platform's culture
- [ ] The CTA fits the platform (save or send on IG, comment on LinkedIn, reply on X and Bluesky, subscribe on YT, join on Telegram)
- [ ] Alt text is written
- [ ] Captions or subtitles are on every video
- [ ] No other platform's watermark
